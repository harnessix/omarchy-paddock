- New: `paddock-host spawn HARNESS --model NAME --effort low|medium|high|xhigh` chooses the model and the reasoning effort of a child, through the harness's own flags (`[oneshot] model_args` and `effort_args` of its profile; codex and claude have them). A harness without them refuses the flags. A Spec Kit task line can carry `[model:NAME]` or `[effort:LEVEL]` for `--task`. `[spawn] effort` sets a default, and `[spawn] models` limits the models a child may get. The approval card, `paddock-host status` and `paddock ps --json` show the model and the effort.
